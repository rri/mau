Add a book, mock 2

Opened from the + in the catalog header. Search results are canned examples; the edition names and ISBNs are made up.

Try these

  • Type "earthsea" and press Enter, then pick a result to see the confirm sheet.
  • Search "le guin": The Left Hand of Darkness is marked "On your list". Picking it opens "add another copy".
  • Type the ISBN 9780000000019 to see ISBN detection, which goes straight to one edition.
  • Search "hollow fern" to see the fallback to a second provider.
  • Tap Scan, then simulate a scan.
  • Search "zzz", then use Enter it manually. Enter the title "Earthsea" with any author and tap Continue to see the similar-titles check.

Approved decisions shown

  • One search box for title, author, or ISBN, with Scan beside it and a manual-entry link below the results. Import lives in Settings.
  • Providers are tried in the admin's order; the next is asked only when results are missing or thin.
  • Live camera scanning with a bundled decoder, and typing the ISBN as the fallback.
  • Picking a result opens a confirm sheet: edition, "I own a copy" (format and location), and tags. Add puts the book at the top of your inactive books.
  • Books already on your list are marked, and picking one offers "add another copy".
  • Manual entry: title and author required; more authors, year, cover, and edition details optional. Private to you until an admin promotes it.
  • Search runs only when you press Enter.
  • "I own a copy" is off unless you tick it.
  • All authors are equals. Translator, editor, and illustrator are separate non-author roles, shown alongside (see "hollow fern").
  • Manual entry checks the catalog for similar titles before saving.
  • Also approved: results are works; the edition picker defaults to the scanned ISBN's edition; you stay on the add screen after Add; the scanner doesn't reopen; ISBNs are validated and stored as ISBN-13; no "Start reading now".
