Approved decisions shown

  • Settings is a list of sections, each its own page. Admin appears only for admins.
  • Several named passkeys; the last can't be removed. Sessions last 30 days and can be revoked.
  • Ten single-use recovery codes, regenerable (voids the old set), with the remaining count shown. An admin-issued recovery link is shown to the user afterwards.
  • Username rules: 3–30 characters (a–z, 0–9, _ and -), changeable, old name reserved for 30 days.
  • Sharing per item (finished, active, a tag, whole catalog). Each item is either Private (not shared) or Public: listed on your public profile (/u/username, with an optional short bio), also reachable by its own link, and indexable by search engines. Sharing with specific people is postponed. Ratings optional, notes never.
  • Import from Goodreads, StoryGraph, LibraryThing, or any CSV, with review before commit. Unfinished books come in inactive. Export as full JSON or simple CSV.
  • Self-service account deletion: type your username, confirm with a passkey, export offered first.
  • Admin: users (promote, suspend, delete, recovery link), signup mode and invites, catalog providers in order, review queue, shared catalog edits, audit log, stats.
