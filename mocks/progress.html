Catalog

    Progress view, styled

    The approved behavior in the chosen look: Paper and print, Newsreader, deep teal, compact rows, "page 140 of 304". Follows your system's light or dark mode.

    Try these

    • Tap + on Middlemarch a few times. Each tap is one page, and the row stays put.
    • Tap the page numbers on Gödel, Escher, Bach. Edit the current page, the total, or both.
    • Tap play on Station Eleven (not started). Setup asks for the page count.
    • Drag Pale Fire up between two inactive books. It stays inactive in its new spot.
    • Drag The Brothers Karamazov up between two active books. It becomes active there.
    • Drop an inactive book right below the last active book. It stays inactive, at the top of the inactive books.
    • Drag an active book down into the inactive books. It stays active and goes to the end of the active list.
    • Tap + on A Wizard of Earthsea (page 182 of 183) to finish it.

    Approved decisions shown

    • One continuous list: active books first, then all other unfinished books.
    • Progress is always in pages. + appears only on active rows and adds 1 page.
    • The first activation of a book asks for its total pages, inline. Confirming starts it at page 0.
    • Tapping the progress edits the current page and the total pages as two separate fields.
    • Active books keep the order you drag them into. Play puts a book at the end of the active books.
    • The whole list is in one manual order, active books first. Nothing reorders itself: editing pages, including on inactive books, never moves a row.
    • Pausing puts a book at the top of the inactive books.
    • Every row has a drag handle. Dropping an inactive book between active books activates it there. Dragging an active book down leaves it active and moves it to the end of the active list. Dropping at the edge between active and inactive changes nothing about either.
    • The undo toast stays up for 10 seconds.
    • Reaching the total finishes the book with an undo toast, and it leaves the list.
